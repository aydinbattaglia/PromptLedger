The problem

Credits are designed to be confusing.

Each tool invents its own unit and hides the exchange rate. You find out what a project cost only after you've spent it.

Runway
credits
A number that means nothing until you divide by your plan's dollar rate.
ElevenLabs
characters
Now rebranded "credits" — priced per character, tracked nowhere per clip.
Midjourney
GPU hours
You buy compute time, not images, and burn it at a rate you can't see.
How it works

Three steps. Then never think about it again.

01

Install & set your plan

Add the extension and pick your plan — or let it read the rate from your billing page automatically.

02

Generate as normal

Keep working. PromptLedger notes each generation's credit change in the background — zero workflow change.

03

Read the ledger

Open the dashboard for cost over time, spend by tool, per-project totals, and a one-click CSV export.

What you get
